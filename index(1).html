About us

Echo Films Uganda is a film production company. We take a story from the first page of the script to the final colour grade, working with small crews and real locations across Uganda.

Our trailers and films are published on our YouTube channel. We also shoot music videos, documentaries and commercial work for clients who want their message to be seen and remembered.
